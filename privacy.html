Privacy Policy

Zhaowei Gu's Assistant · Last updated October 23, 2026

Who uses this app

Zhaowei Gu's Assistant is a personal tool. I am its only user, and it connects only to my own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With my permission, it uses Google's APIs to read and update my Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Calendar, and to read my Contacts.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out tasks I ask for. It is processed on my computer and by the AI model service that powers the assistant. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Removing access

I can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
